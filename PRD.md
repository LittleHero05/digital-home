# Digital Home: PRD (v1)

## Why
A personal space that feels like flipping through my notebook. A home for my projects, writing, and photos, not a portfolio pitch. Visitors should feel: warm, handmade, curious, calm.

## Metaphor
**Bullet journal / notebook.**
- The home page is a spread: *today's log* on the left, the *index* (table of contents) on the right. The index doubles as the menu.
- Every section is a *spread*: two facing pages with a spine in the middle.

## Pages (v1)
| Route | Purpose | Spread |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Home: today's log + index | Left: date, greeting + polaroid, log entries (bujo signifiers), curiosity tracker. Right: "Index" contents list (signifier, section, dotted leader, page number) |
| `/projects` | Things I've built | Left: title + blurb. Right: project cards (clipped/taped notes) |
| `/gallery` | Pictures | Left: title + blurb. Right: photo grid (taped-in prints) |
| `/blog` | Writing index | Left: title + blurb. Right: list of posts |
| `/blog/[slug]` | A post (text + images) | Left: title, date, tags, cover. Right: body |
| `/about` | Who I am | Left: photo + short bio. Right: "currently" list + links |
| `/sudoku` | Today's puzzle | Left: title, stats (time / filled / mistakes), number pad, tools, mini curiosity tracker. Right: the 9×9 grid |

## Navigation
- Home: the index lists **projects, gallery, blog, about** as real links. Each row has a bujo signifier (● project, ○ gallery, – blog, ★ about) that means the same thing across the site.
- Inner pages: breadcrumb back to home, plus a compact text nav.
- Must work by keyboard.

## Sudoku & curiosity tracker
- The home page shows a small 3×3 **curiosity tracker**. Its "fill today's →" link opens `/sudoku`.
- `/sudoku` shows **one puzzle per day**, the same for every visitor, picked by date. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.
- **Grid:** given numbers in bold ink; the visitor's numbers in blue serif italic ("handwriting"); pencil marks as small blue numbers. The selected square is yellow, with its row, column and 3×3 box lightly highlighted.
- **Number pad:** nine circles (1–9). No "left" counts; a number is faded and crossed out once all nine of it are placed.
- **Tools:** pencil marks on/off, erase, undo, "check my work".
- **Solved:** the title changes to "Solved it.", a SOLVED stamp with the date appears, and today's square in the tracker fills in.
- **Tracker complete (9/9):** a small, one-time animation plays; the tracker then stays filled. No reset, no rewards.
- **Puzzles:** generated by our own script (no third-party puzzles, so no licensing questions) and stored in `/content/sudoku.ts`. Each puzzle is checked for a unique solution.
- **Progress** (entries in progress, tracker squares) is saved in the visitor's browser (`localStorage`). No accounts, no database.
- Must work by keyboard: arrow keys move, 1–9 enter a number, Backspace erases.

## Style
**Palette** (tokens, starting values, tweak by eye)
- `paper` `#FBFAF6`: off-white page background
- `dot` `#DAD7CC`: dot-grid marks on the paper
- `yellow` `#FFF3B0`: pastel yellow, used for fills, highlighter swipes, washi tape, sticky notes
- `blue` `#1D3FAE`: bright navy, used for links, borders, pen-style lines, accents
- `ink` `#1B1F3B`: body text

Pastel yellow is a fill or highlight color only, never text color.

**Type**
- Helvetica (`"Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) for most things: body, headings, UI
- Source Serif 4 for accents: dates, captions, pull quotes, margin notes, italic "handwritten note" moments

**Journal details** (keep them light)
- Dot-grid paper background
- Spine/gutter between the two pages of a spread
- Washi-tape strips, slightly rotated cards (±1–2°)
- Highlighter-swipe underline on headings
- Thin navy pen-style borders and simple hand-drawn-feeling SVG arrows/underlines
- Motion is subtle (small hover wobble, gentle page-in). Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

**Responsive**
- ≥1024px: two-page spread
- <1024px: the two pages stack into one column

## Content model
- Blog: MDX files in `/content/blog/*.mdx` with frontmatter (`title`, `date`, `tags`, `cover`, `summary`). Images in `/public/blog/`.
- Projects: typed array in `/content/projects.ts` (`title`, `blurb`, `tags`, `image`, `links`).
- Gallery: images in `/public/gallery/` plus a typed array in `/content/gallery.ts` (`src`, `alt`, `caption`, `date`).
- No CMS, no database. Content lives in git.

## Tech
Next.js (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind, `next/font` (Source Serif 4), `next/image`, MDX (`gray-matter` + `next-mdx-remote`, or simplest equivalent). Deploy on Vercel.

## Out of scope for v1
Dark mode, search, comments, RSS, newsletter, CMS, analytics, page-turn animations, per-project detail pages, gallery lightbox, i18n.

## Done means
- All five sections reachable from the home index and live on Vercel
- `/sudoku` reachable from the home tracker, with a solvable daily puzzle that saves progress
- One real blog post and a few real photos/projects in place
- Usable on a phone
- Looks like a notebook, not a template
